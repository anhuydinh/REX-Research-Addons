# REX-Research-Addons

Additional studies inspired by work done during the UBC URO REX Experience.

## Overview

This repository contains follow-up work and additional studies building on the research conducted during the UBC Undergraduate Research Experience (REX) program.

The REX project focuses on the **Pan-tissue human organoid single-cell RNA atlas** — a systematic review of publicly available single-cell and single-nucleus RNA sequencing data from human organoid models across tissues.

This repository is for exploratory and supplementary analyses developed alongside that work.

## Background

Human organoids are stem-cell-derived 3D models that recapitulate key features of human tissues. Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) and single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) make it possible to profile cellular diversity within these models at single-cell resolution.

The REX project reviews published organoid studies, verifies the public availability of their sc/snRNA-seq data, and organizes the findings by tissue — including retina, brain, kidney, heart, and liver.
